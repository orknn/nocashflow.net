CRYPTO

Ahead of the Fed, is the crypto selloff driven by leverage or institutional outflows?

Total crypto market capitalization fell 4.38% in 24 hours while Bitcoin and Ethereum ETFs recorded net outflows of $450.4 million and $142.3 million, respectively; at the same time, Bitcoin open interest rose 16.23%, Ethereum open interest rose 7.11%, and the Coinbase premium fell to -0.126. The research question is whether the decline reflects weak spot demand or forced unwinding of derivatives leverage; monitor Bitcoin’s reaction at 75,476 support and 77,937 resistance, a reversal in ETF flows, and whether open interest falls alongside price.
