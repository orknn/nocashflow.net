Could the Kharg tension become an oil shock?

Iranian statements about the Kharg energy hub put the transmission from geopolitical risk to physical oil supply and European equities back in focus. The research question is whether the episode creates a lasting supply disruption or only a temporary risk premium; key signals are EIA weekly inventory, production and trade data alongside energy-sector market performance.

  • Reuters — Europe stocks edge lower as oil jumps; monthly gain in sight - Reuters — The initial market transmission from higher oil prices into European indexes and sectors.
MACRO

Will September labor and PMI data reset Fed pricing?

The U.S. calendar from September 1–4 delivers a concentrated sequence of signals on the growth-labor balance through ISM manufacturing and services PMIs, JOLTS, ADP and payrolls; previous and forecast readings include 55.6 versus 55.2 for manufacturing PMI, 7.36M versus 7.33M for JOLTS, and -23K versus 58K for payrolls. The research question is how the data alter the 3.8 2026 median rate path and risk appetite ahead of the September 16 FOMC; focus on upside or downside surprises in payrolls, wages and the PMI mix.
Primary Sources / Reports:
  • ISM — Report on Business (PMI) — The September 1 and 3 manufacturing and services PMI readings, especially employment, new orders and prices subindexes.
  • BLS — Employment Situation (NFP) — The September 4 payrolls, unemployment rate and average hourly earnings composition versus expectations.
  • Federal Reserve — FOMC Calendar & Statements — The official September 16 FOMC schedule, decision and statement language to compare with the current 3.8 pricing.

What do ETF divergence and leverage fragility reveal in crypto?

Bitcoin ETFs recorded total outflows of $201.9 million on August 28 and the Coinbase premium fell to -0.0502, while Ethereum ETFs attracted $102.1 million, raising the question of internal capital rotation. The research question is whether this divergence reflects durable spot demand or temporary leverage-driven positioning; monitor continued ETF flows, BTC’s 77,629 support and 79,924 resistance, and SOL’s 0.6648 funding rate.

  • Farside — Bitcoin ETF Flows (All Data) — The fund-level breakdown of the August 28 total outflow of $201.9 million and whether flows reverse in subsequent sessions.
  • Farside — Ethereum ETF Flows — The allocation and persistence of the $102.1 million total inflow between ETHA and FETH.
  • Coinglass — Derivatives Data — Open interest, funding and liquidation data for BTC, ETH and SOL, with emphasis on whether SOL’s 0.6648 funding rate signals an extreme.
