RISK-ON A softer DXY, a stronger copper-to-gold ratio and Greed-level sentiment are supporting risk appetite despite geopolitical risks.

The risk-on regime remains intact, supported by a weaker dollar and strength in the copper-to-gold signal. Despite short-term volatility in crypto, BTC has recovered on a weekly basis, while ETH, XRP, and selected altcoins show stronger momentum. The Fear and Greed indicator remains in the greed zone, pointing to constructive investor sentiment. However, weakness in some major technology stocks and broader intraday pressure call for cautious optimism toward the upside trend.

COMMODITIES

Hormuz and Saudi energy infrastructure: can the oil-supply shock become persistent?

Slower Hormuz traffic and attacks on Saudi energy facilities test how far geopolitical risk is reaching physical oil flows and importing economies. The key signal is confirmation of deterioration in supply and inventories, alongside whether pressure on the rupee and Indian equities intensifies.
Primary Sources / Reports:

  • EIA — Weekly Petroleum Status Report — US crude and product inventories, production, imports, and refinery utilization for confirmation of a supply shock.
MACRO

Is the oil shock resetting inflation and central-bank pricing?

With oil nearing a six-week high, the 11 September US CPI release matters not only for core inflation but also for energy pass-through; eight days before the Fed meeting, markets may price inflation and growth risks together. Watch whether CPI deviates from the 0.4% headline and 0.2% core monthly forecasts and how CME FedWatch reprices rates.
Primary Sources / Reports:
  • BLS — Consumer Price Index (CPI) — Compare the 11 September headline and core monthly/yearly CPI figures with forecasts.
  • CME FedWatch Tool — Track how FOMC rate probabilities are repriced after CPI and PPI.
  • Federal Reserve — FOMC Calendar & Statements — Review the 16 September FOMC schedule and, when released, its inflation-risk messaging.
CRYPTO

Are ETF inflows masking fragility in crypto derivatives?

Bitcoin ETFs saw $174.6 million of inflows and Ethereum ETFs $25.9 million, yet total crypto market capitalization fell 3.15% in 24 hours; rising ETH and SOL open interest suggests leverage is concentrating. The research question is whether spot demand can absorb that risk appetite: monitor Bitcoin’s $77,496.4 support and $80,514.4 resistance, and whether the Coinbase premium recovers from -0.0245.

  • Farside — Bitcoin ETF Flows (All Data) — Check whether daily total Bitcoin ETF inflows and the IBIT/FBTC contributions continue.
  • Farside — Ethereum ETF Flows — Track whether the Ethereum ETF total and the divergence between ETHA and FETH flows persist.
  • Coinglass — Derivatives Data — Monitor BTC, ETH, and SOL open interest, funding rates, leverage buildup, and liquidation risk.
