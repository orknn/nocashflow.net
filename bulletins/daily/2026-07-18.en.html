RISK-OFF The 7th night of Iran-US conflict, surging VIX, and tech selloff crush risk appetite across markets.

Global markets shifted firmly into risk-off mode as the US military completed its 7th consecutive night of strikes on Iran, while Iran renewed attacks on Gulf states. VIX surged 12.2% to 18.77 and NASDAQ 100 Futures fell 1.55%, with every Magnificent 7 stock closing in the red. Geopolitical tensions rattled commodity markets: Brent crude jumped 4.6% to $88.10 while gold held firm at $4,012. In crypto, BTC trades at $63,951 while the Fear & Greed Index sits at 25 (Extreme Fear), confirming broad market anxiety. BTC dominance at 56.4% remains elevated as altcoins underperform, reinforcing the flight-to-quality dynamic within crypto. On the macro calendar, this week's US CPI print on July 14 is front and center — consensus expects headline inflation to ease from 4.2% to 3.8% YoY, though geopolitically driven energy price spikes leave this forecast vulnerable to an upside surprise.
