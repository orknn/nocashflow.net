Global Macro & Crypto Market Intelligence
Fri, Sep 18 2026
RISK-ON Risk appetite is strong: subdued volatility, firmer equity futures and a broad crypto rally support a risk-on regime.

Global markets remain in a risk-on posture, with Nasdaq 100 futures up 1.37% and SMH gaining 2.76% as the VIX eased to 15.34. The US 10-year Treasury yield fell 5.9 basis points to 4.947%, while the DXY edged higher to 100.349. In crypto, Bitcoin rose 2.40% over 24 hours to $78,124 and Ethereum gained 3.10%; total market capitalization stands at $2.69 trillion, Bitcoin dominance at 58.13% and Fear & Greed at 56, classified as Greed. Broad-based altcoin gains and a 0.08% 24-hour increase in combined stablecoin market capitalization suggest that liquidity demand remains firm. Strong retail sales released on September 16, including a 1.4% rise in core retail sales, point to resilient US consumption; no actual Fed rate decision or FOMC statement is provided for the listed September 16 event, while the next FOMC meeting is scheduled for October 28 with a median rate projection of 4.1%.

COMMODITIES

Hormuz traffic and the durability of the oil-supply risk premium

Traffic through the Strait of Hormuz remains below its 10-day average while LNG vessels have reappeared, and oil prices fell 2% as fears of a Saudi supply disruption eased. The research question is how much geopolitical risk premium the market has removed; the key signals are whether tanker/LNG traffic normalizes and whether US inventory and production data confirm a physical supply shock.
Primary Sources / Reports:
  • Reuters — Hormuz traffic below 10-day average, LNG vessels reappear outside strait - Reuters — Track daily tanker and LNG passages through Hormuz, normalization signals, and potential supply disruptions relative to the 10-day average.
  • EIA — Weekly Petroleum Status Report — Check crude, gasoline and distillate inventories, refinery utilization, imports, exports and production for physical confirmation of a supply shock.
  • Reuters — Oil prices fall 2% on easing fears over Saudi supply disruption - Reuters — Examine the market rationale for reduced Saudi supply-disruption fears and the supply assumption behind the price decline.
MACRO

The tension between resilient US consumption and the rate path

US retail sales beat expectations while jobless claims came in below forecast, even as the 10-year Treasury yield stood at 4.947% and the DXY at 100.349. The research question is whether resilient growth is consistent with the Fed’s 4.1% median rate projection and at what yield threshold renewed rate pressure would challenge equity valuations.
Primary Sources / Reports:
  • FRED — St. Louis Fed Economic Data — Check the trend and revisions in retail-sales series RSAFS and RSFSXMV, together with initial claims series ICSA.
  • Federal Reserve — FOMC Calendar & Statements — Review the September 16 FOMC statement and projections for the policy-rate path, growth and inflation assessment, and forward guidance.
  • US Treasury — Daily Yield Curve Rates — Monitor the 4.947% 10-year yield and whether the broader daily curve is repricing across maturities.
CRYPTO

The collision between Bitcoin spot-ETF demand and leveraged positioning

Bitcoin ETFs recorded total inflows of $159.5 million on September 17 while Ethereum ETFs saw $39.3 million of outflows; at the same time, BTC and ETH open interest rose and the Coinbase premium remained at -0.0758. The research question is whether spot demand reflects genuine accumulation or a fragile, derivatives-led rally; monitor whether the negative premium persists and whether BTC holds the 76,577.995 support or clears the 79,039.01 resistance.
Primary Sources / Reports:
  • Farside — Bitcoin ETF Flows (All Data) — Break down the September 17 total inflow of $159.5 million by fund, especially IBIT and FBTC, and assess persistence.
  • Farside — Ethereum ETF Flows — Track the sources of the September 17 total outflow of $39.3 million, including ETHA and other funds, and look for reversal signals.
  • Coinglass — Derivatives Data — Check the rise in BTC and ETH open interest, funding rates, liquidations, and leverage concentration around the 76,577.995 support and 79,039.01 resistance levels.
