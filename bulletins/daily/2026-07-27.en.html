Mon, Jul 27 2026
NEUTRAL US-Iran ceasefire sparks a relief rally, but rising yields and ETF outflows keep the broader picture balanced.

Global markets staged a modest relief rally after the US and Iran agreed to pause hostilities, though the macro backdrop continues to send mixed signals. VIX fell 5.8% to 17.62, and Asian equities and bonds bounced; however, the US 10-Year Treasury yield climbed to 4.70%, its highest level in recent weeks, maintaining pressure on risk assets. The DXY edged down to 101.26, offering limited relief. All eyes this week are on the FOMC rate decision on July 29, with the Fed widely expected to hold rates at 3.75%. Additionally, Core PCE (forecast: 0.1% MoM) and Advance GDP (forecast: 2.3%) due on July 30 will be pivotal for the Fed's forward guidance. In crypto, BTC trades around $65,130 while the Fear & Greed Index sits at 30 (Fear), and total crypto market cap hovers near $2.32 trillion.

MACRO

FOMC Decision 48 Hours Away: Markets Price 'Hold', But Bond Yields Stay Elevated

With the July 29 FOMC meeting just two days away, the Fed funds rate is expected to remain at 3.75%, yet the 10-year Treasury yield hovers near 4.70% and the 2s10s spread sits at only 33 basis points. The same week's Advance GDP (2.3% forecast) and Core PCE (0.1% MoM forecast) due on July 30 will directly shape the Fed's forward guidance. Key signal to watch: whether the FOMC statement includes language confirming 'inflation on target' and whether the 2s10s spread breaks below 30 basis points.

Crude Oil Futures Price 'Market Adaptability', Not Peace: Where Is the Real Risk?

While the US-Iran ceasefire news boosted risk appetite in equities and bonds, Reuters analysis suggests crude oil futures are pricing market adaptability to supply disruption rather than a durable peace scenario. Gold's 1%+ gain signals that geopolitical uncertainty has not fully dissipated. Key signal to watch: EIA weekly crude inventory data and Gulf production variables — an unexpected inventory build would reinforce the 'supply resilience' thesis, while a draw would suggest geopolitical risk premium is still embedded in prices.

Bitcoin ETF Outflow Streak and Persistent Negative Coinbase Premium: Where Are the Institutional Buyers?

The Coinbase premium has remained consistently negative over the past week, signaling weakening US institutional demand. The Fear & Greed Index stands at 30 (Fear) and BTC is in a bearish trend on the 4-hour chart. Key signal to watch: whether ETF outflows exceed $100M for three consecutive days, or whether the Coinbase premium flips positive.
