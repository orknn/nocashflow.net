Global Macro & Crypto Market Intelligence
Mon, Aug 10 2026
NEUTRAL The market outlook remains neutral: low volatility and ETF inflows support risk appetite, but fear, geopolitical risks, and inflation uncertainty keep the balance in check.

Global markets remain balanced as Nasdaq 100 futures rose 0.28% and SMH gained 1.96%, while the VIX fell 1.65% to 14.90, indicating limited near-term stress. The 10-year U.S. Treasury yield eased to 4.66% while the 2-year yield rose to 4.25%, leaving the 2s10s spread at 0.41 percentage points; the DXY edged 0.09% higher to 99.69. Total crypto market capitalization increased 0.41% to $2.31 trillion, with BTC up 0.5% at $65,221, but the Fear & Greed reading remains in the Fear zone at 30 and BTC dominance stands at 56.70%. U.S. CPI due on August 12 is forecast at 3.4% year over year for headline inflation and 2.5% for core inflation, while PPI on August 13 will also be important for Fed expectations. Gold rose 1.72% while Brent was broadly flat; Middle East supply risks and the upcoming inflation data keep the neutral regime vulnerable to a directional break.

MACRO

What inflation scenario is the Treasury market pricing ahead of CPI?

Ahead of the U.S. CPI release on August 12, the 10-year yield has fallen to 4.66% while the 2-year yield has risen to 4.25%, highlighting tension between longer-term growth/risk views and near-term Fed expectations. The research question is how a core CPI reading above or below the 0.2% monthly forecast would affect front-end rates, the 99.69 DXY level and CME FedWatch pricing; the key threshold is the deviation from 0.2% month over month.
Primary Sources / Reports:
  • BLS — Consumer Price Index (CPI) — Review headline and core monthly/yearly CPI results on August 12, focusing on the deviation from the 0.2% core monthly forecast.
  • US Treasury — Daily Yield Curve Rates — Track post-CPI moves in 2-year and 10-year yields and changes from the 0.41% 2s10s spread.
  • CME FedWatch Tool — Check how the CPI surprise changes rate expectations and probability distributions for the next FOMC meeting.

Is the crypto rally driven by institutional flows or leverage?

Total crypto market capitalization rose 0.41% over 24 hours, while Bitcoin and Ethereum ETFs recorded net inflows of $101.7 million and $49.6 million respectively; however, the Coinbase premium fell to -0.135%, pointing to weaker U.S. spot demand. The research question is whether ETF buying is persistent enough to support prices; key levels are a continuing negative Coinbase premium, BTC’s $63,786.84 support and whether ETH funding rises from 0.0128%.
