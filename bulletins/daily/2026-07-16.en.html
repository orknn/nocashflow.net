RISK-OFF Geopolitical pressure from the Iran war and 'Extreme Fear' sentiment push markets into a defensive posture.

Markets are trading today against a backdrop of escalating US-Iran tensions, with declining vessel traffic through the Strait of Hormuz keeping energy market uncertainty elevated. Brent crude holds at $84.75, having surged over 11% in the past seven days, reflecting the significant geopolitical risk premium priced into oil. On the macro front, VIX stands at 15.80 while the US 10Y Treasury yield fell to 4.545%, down roughly 4 basis points on the day — a signal of growing demand for safe-haven assets. DXY continues to soften at 100.47. In crypto, the Fear & Greed Index sits deep in Extreme Fear at 25, with BTC trading at $64,208, down 0.53% over 24 hours; BTC dominance at 56.1% confirms risk-off sentiment is prevailing. This week's US CPI release (annual forecast 3.8% vs prior 4.2%) on Tuesday and PPI data on Wednesday will be critical inputs shaping expectations ahead of the July 29 FOMC meeting, with markets closely watching for any shift in the Fed's rate path.
