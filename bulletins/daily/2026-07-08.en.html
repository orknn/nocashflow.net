RISK-OFF Escalating US-Iran tensions and tanker attacks in the Strait of Hormuz have brought geopolitical risk premium back to the forefront.

Global markets are trading under heavy geopolitical pressure today as US strikes on Iran and Iran's retaliatory attacks on targets in Bahrain and Kuwait pushed Middle East tensions past a critical threshold. BTC holds a 7-day gain of +7.1% but slipped -0.35% over the past 24 hours to $62,822, with the Fear & Greed Index firmly in Extreme Fear (20) territory. On the macro side, the US 10-year Treasury yield climbed to 4.53% while VIX at 16.43 looks deceptively calm — the Energy sector (XLE +2.84%) and Brent crude ($75.79, +2.20%) are already pricing in a geopolitical risk premium. Tonight's FOMC Meeting Minutes at 21:00 ET represent the session's key macro event; any hawkish signals on the rate path could further dampen risk appetite. The DXY at 100.97 remains relatively soft, and tomorrow's Unemployment Claims will also be closely watched.
