RISK-ON A softer dollar and lower VIX, combined with strong crypto performance and ETF inflows, are supporting risk appetite.

The market backdrop supports a risk-on regime: VIX fell to 15.32, DXY declined 0.25% to 101.04, and Nasdaq 100 futures gained 0.53%. The US 10-year Treasury yield rose 4.80 basis points to 5.162%, showing that rate sensitivity remains elevated despite the constructive risk tone. In crypto, BTC rose 1.83% in 24 hours to $84,703, while ETH gained 2.92%, XRP 6.86%, and SOL 5.30%; total market capitalization stands at $2.88 trillion, BTC dominance at 58.93%, and Fear & Greed at 71, or Greed. However, total crypto market capitalization slipped 0.73% over 24 hours, while the Coinbase premium at -0.029% indicates that strong US spot demand has not yet been fully confirmed. Today’s calendar includes revised University of Michigan consumer sentiment and inflation expectations; with no CPI, PPI, PCE, or FOMC decision released this week, the key macro risks are elevated Treasury yields and the 4.1% median rate path ahead of the October 28 FOMC meeting.
