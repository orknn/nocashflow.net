RISK-OFF Closure of the Strait of Hormuz instantly reprices geopolitical risk premium across global markets.

As of July 12, 2026, markets are absorbing a severe geopolitical shock following U.S. military strikes against Iran and the IRGC's announcement that the Strait of Hormuz is closed until further notice. Despite the magnitude of the event, Brent crude trading at $76.01 suggests markets may not have fully priced in the supply disruption risk. On the crypto front, BTC sits at $63,814, down 0.46% over 24 hours, while the Fear & Greed Index registers a reading of 26 (Fear), confirming that geopolitical uncertainty is weighing heavily on risk appetite. On the macro side, the 10-year U.S. Treasury yield climbed to 4.57% while the 2-year yield fell to 4.16%, producing a +41 basis point 2s10s spread that signals a steepening yield curve. Critically, this Tuesday's July 14 CPI release (YoY forecast: 3.8%, prior: 4.2%) will be a pivotal event; the risk that the Hormuz crisis reignites energy-driven inflation could push back Fed rate cut expectations significantly.
