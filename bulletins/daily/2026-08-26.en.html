RISK-ON Falling VIX and MOVE, strong ETF inflows, and Greed-level sentiment indicate that risk appetite remains intact.

Total crypto market capitalization stood at $2.675 trillion but fell 4.45% over 24 hours; BTC traded at $79,036, down 2.07% daily despite a 23% seven-day gain, with BTC dominance at 59.28% and Fear & Greed at 65, or Greed. Today’s calendar highlights core PCE, forecast at 0.2% month over month, and preliminary GDP, forecast at 1.5%; until these figures are released, rate expectations and risk assets remain highly data-sensitive.
