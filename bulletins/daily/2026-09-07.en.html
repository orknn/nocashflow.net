Global Macro & Crypto Market Intelligence
Mon, Sep 7 2026
NEUTRAL Mixed macro signals, rising geopolitical risk and cautious crypto flows are keeping markets in a neutral balance.

The market backdrop remains neutral as supportive and defensive signals offset one another. The VIX rose 3.44% to 15.03, while the DXY fell 0.22%, limiting the tightening pressure from the dollar; U.S. 2-year and 10-year Treasury yields were unavailable, so the curve cannot be assessed. Total crypto market capitalization fell 3.12% over 24 hours to $2.69 trillion, while BTC declined 0.72%; nevertheless, BTC remains up 1.10% over seven days and 22.10% over 30 days. The Fear & Greed index stayed in the Greed zone at 71, while the Coinbase premium fell to -0.0414%, pointing to cautious near-term U.S. spot demand. U.S. PPI is due on September 10 and CPI on September 11; with headline CPI expectations at 0.4% month-on-month and 3.4% year-on-year, and core CPI at 2.4% year-on-year, energy costs and diesel prices are key inflation risks. The next FOMC decision is scheduled for September 16, with the current 2026 Fed dots median at 3.8%.

STRATEGIC RESEARCH FOCUS
COMMODITIES

Repricing the Gulf’s energy routes

Iran’s plan to announce a new restricted zone in the Gulf and the UAE’s effort to build alternative trade and energy routes make it necessary to investigate whether physical supply risk is becoming persistent. The key signal is whether the zone’s scope and the real capacity of alternative routes feed into diesel and crude prices, especially US inflation expectations.
Primary Sources / Reports:
  • Reuters — UAE builds alternative trade, energy routes after Iran attacks - reuters.com — Examine which alternative trade and energy routes the UAE is developing, including their capacity and timing.
  • Reuters — Iran says to announce new restricted zone in the Gulf in the coming days - reuters.com — Track the restricted zone’s geographic scope, effective timing and operational constraints on maritime transport.
  • EIA — Weekly Petroleum Status Report — Check weekly US crude, refinery utilization and distillate/diesel inventory changes.
MACRO

Can a diesel shock disrupt Fed pricing ahead of PPI and CPI?

Diesel’s role in the US inflation outlook requires investigating how sensitive rate-cut expectations are to a supply shock ahead of PPI on September 10 and CPI on September 11. The concrete threshold is whether PPI exceeds its 0.4% monthly forecast and headline CPI exceeds its 0.4% monthly forecast, alongside Treasury and dollar reactions and CME FedWatch pricing before the September 16 FOMC meeting.
Primary Sources / Reports:
  • Reuters — Morning Bid: Diesel the real spoiler for US inflation doves - reuters.com — Examine diesel’s transmission channels into the US inflation basket and the risks to rate-cut expectations.
  • BLS — Consumer Price Index (CPI) — Check whether September 11 headline CPI deviates from the 0.4% monthly and 3.4% annual forecasts, and whether core CPI deviates from the 0.2% monthly and 2.4% annual forecasts.
  • CME FedWatch Tool — Track changes in futures-implied rate probabilities for the September 16 FOMC meeting after the PPI and CPI releases.
CRYPTO

Are ETF inflows accumulating alongside derivatives fragility?

Bitcoin and Ethereum ETFs recorded net inflows of $174.6 million and $25.9 million respectively on September 4, while total crypto market capitalization fell 3.12% over 24 hours; BTC and ETH funding was negative while SOL funding remained positive. The research question is whether spot demand can absorb leveraged-position unwinding; key levels are BTC support at $77,851, resistance at $80,869, and the direction of the Coinbase premium from its current negative 0.0414% reading.
Primary Sources / Reports:
  • Farside — Bitcoin ETF Flows (All Data) — Check whether daily Bitcoin ETF inflows from IBIT, FBTC and the total remain consistent with the $174.6 million net inflow.
  • Farside — Ethereum ETF Flows — Examine how the $25.9 million total Ethereum ETF inflow was distributed across funds and whether the flow persists.
  • Coinglass — Derivatives Data — Monitor BTC, ETH and SOL funding rates and open interest to determine whether negative BTC/ETH funding and positive SOL funding are turning into leveraged unwinding.
