Global Macro & Crypto Market Intelligence
Tue, Jul 21 2026
NEUTRAL Crypto prices climb amid geopolitical uncertainty and Extreme Fear, keeping markets in neutral territory.

Global markets are generating mixed signals as US-Iran tensions enter their 10th consecutive night of strikes and Strait of Hormuz vessel crossings continue to decline. Hopes for Mideast mediation have pulled oil prices lower, supporting a bounce in Asian equities, while gold surged 1.6% to $4,076, continuing to price in geopolitical risk premiums. On the macro front, VIX has eased to 17.59, yet the 10-year US Treasury yield has climbed to 4.60%, maintaining pressure on equity valuations, with DXY hovering around 100.9. In crypto, BTC reached $66,158 — up 5.8% on the week — and total market cap stands at $2.34 trillion, yet the Fear & Greed Index remains firmly in 'Extreme Fear' territory at 25. This divergence between rising prices and depressed sentiment suggests the rally lacks broad conviction. Looking ahead, the ECB rate decision on July 23 (expected unchanged at 2.40%) and US Jobless Claims will serve as key near-term catalysts.
