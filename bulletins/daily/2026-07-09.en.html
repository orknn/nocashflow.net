NEUTRAL Markets seek neutral equilibrium caught between chip euphoria and escalating geopolitical tensions.

Markets are navigating conflicting signals today: the semiconductor rally led by NVIDIA's +3.65% surge and SMH ETF's +1.99% gain partially support risk appetite, while geopolitical uncertainty stemming from U.S. strikes on Iran and Trump's halt of trade with Spain keep nerves on edge. On the macro front, the 10-year U.S. Treasury yield continues climbing to 4.57%, while the VIX holds relatively calm at 16.73; the DXY edges lower to 100.93, providing a tailwind for gold and commodities. In crypto, Bitcoin holds around $62,860, but the Fear & Greed Index at 22 (Extreme Fear) signals highly fragile investor sentiment. BTC dominance remains firm at 56.1% with total crypto market cap near $2.25 trillion. Key events this week include the FOMC Meeting Minutes (July 8) and Unemployment Claims (July 9); with the next Fed meeting just 20 days away, any hawkish signals in the minutes could directly pressure risk assets.
