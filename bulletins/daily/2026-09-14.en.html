DAILY FINANCIAL BULLETIN

Global Macro & Crypto Market Intelligence
Mon, Sep 14 2026
NEUTRAL Macro caution and a selective crypto rebound are offsetting each other, keeping the market outlook neutral.

Market conditions remain neutral as higher risk indicators are balanced by a short-term rebound in selected crypto assets. The VIX rose 11.74% to 17.70 and the DXY gained 0.45% to 99.57, while Nasdaq 100 futures declined 1.54%. Brent crude climbed 2.58% to $107.31, keeping geopolitical and energy-supply risks relevant for inflation and growth. Total crypto market capitalization fell to $2.65 trillion, down 1.30% over 24 hours; BTC dominance stood at 58.91%, while Fear & Greed remained in Greed territory at 57. BTC gained 1.63% over 24 hours but was down 1.60% over seven days, while the Coinbase premium fell to -0.0877 and spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded $13.2 million in net outflows. The September 16 Federal Funds Rate decision, FOMC statement and projections, together with retail-sales data due the same day, are the key near-term catalysts.

STRATEGIC RESEARCH FOCUS
COMMODITIES

Is the Strait of Hormuz risk becoming a persistent oil supply shock?

The Iran war, the postponement of diplomacy over the Strait of Hormuz and rising shipping costs may signal a lasting supply and logistics shock rather than a brief price spike. The key research signal is whether EIA inventories and product supply deteriorate while diplomatic efforts remain stalled.
Primary Sources / Reports:
  • Reuters — Morning Bid: Shipping oil gets ever harder, costlier - Reuters — Examine the changes in freight, insurance and oil-shipping costs highlighted by the report, and identify the routes facing the greatest risk.
  • EIA — Weekly Petroleum Status Report — Check US crude and product inventories, refinery utilization, imports and exports to determine whether a physical supply squeeze is emerging.
  • Reuters — Diplomacy stumbles with postponement of meeting on Strait of Hormuz proposal - Reuters — Track why the Hormuz proposal meeting was postponed, when the next diplomatic contact may occur and whether concrete transit-security mechanisms emerge.
MACRO

Are the FOMC, an oil shock and growth risks being priced together?

The September 16 FOMC meeting, a 4.00% federal-funds-rate forecast and a 3.8 median dot will test how an oil-supply risk feeds into inflation and consumption. The key threshold is whether retail sales versus the 0.8% forecast and core sales versus the 0.5% forecast change the market’s interpretation of the FOMC projections.
Primary Sources / Reports:
  • Federal Reserve — FOMC Calendar & Statements — Read the September 16 policy decision, statement and projections for changes in the inflation-growth-employment balance.
  • CME FedWatch Tool — Track how interest-rate futures price the 4.00% expectation before and after the FOMC and how expectations for later meetings move.
  • BLS — Consumer Price Index (CPI) — Examine the direct effect of oil and energy prices on consumer inflation, including the energy component and headline-core divergence.
CRYPTO

What do the ETF divergence and negative Coinbase premium say about crypto demand?

A $13.2 million net outflow from Bitcoin ETFs, a $216.4 million net inflow into Ethereum ETFs and a Coinbase premium falling to -0.0877 point to selective risk appetite within crypto. The research question is whether institutional demand can remain strong in ETH while BTC weakens toward the 76,992.985 support level, and whether elevated SOL funding creates leverage fragility.
Primary Sources / Reports:
  • Farside — Bitcoin ETF Flows (All Data) — Check the September 11 total Bitcoin ETF flow, the distribution across IBIT and other funds, and whether outflows persist on subsequent days.
  • Farside — Ethereum ETF Flows — Examine the fund-level composition and persistence of the $216.4 million Ethereum ETF inflow on September 11.
  • Coinglass — Derivatives Data — Track BTC, ETH and especially SOL funding rates and open interest to assess leverage buildup, liquidations and BTC’s reaction to the 76,992.985 support and 78,914.695 resistance levels.
