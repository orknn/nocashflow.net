NEUTRAL Macro uncertainty and a tech pullback keep markets in neutral territory, while ETF inflows limit downside risk.

Global markets are navigating conflicting signals today. On the macro front, the 10-year US Treasury yield eased to 4.617% but remains elevated, while the 2Y-10Y spread at 41.7 bps stays in positive territory, alleviating near-term recession concerns. MACRO

Pre-NFP Jitters: What Is the Market Pricing as the 2-Year Treasury Yield Drops Sharply?

The US 2-year Treasury yield fell to 4.20% today (daily change: -1.18%), while the 10-year yield holds at 4.617%. This move reflects rising uncertainty ahead of tomorrow's (August 7) Non-Farm Payrolls release; the forecast is 85,000 vs. the prior reading of 57,000. Such a sharp drop in the short end may be pricing in a stronger probability of a Fed rate cut at the September 2026 FOMC meeting. Key signal to watch: whether the probability of a September cut on CME FedWatch exceeds 50%, and whether the 2s10s spread (currently 41.7 bps) widens post-NFP.
Primary Sources / Reports:

  • US Treasury — Daily Yield Curve Rates — Monitor the 2s10s spread on the daily yield curve data before and after the NFP release.
COMMODITIES

Sinopec Pivots to Russian Oil: How Do Middle East Supply Cuts and Iran Talks Reshape the Oil Balance?

According to an exclusive Reuters report, Sinopec is stepping up Russian oil imports to offset supply cuts from the Middle East. Simultaneously, Trump continues to portray Iran talks positively, while Asian equities fell and the energy sector (XLE) was today's worst performer at -2.07%. The intersection of these two dynamics is critical: a potential Iran deal could boost global supply, while Sinopec's shift to Russian crude could weigh on Middle Eastern pricing. Key signal to watch: EIA weekly crude inventory change and the price spread between Russian and Gulf crude.
Primary Sources / Reports:
  • EIA — Weekly Petroleum Status Report — Review the weekly crude oil inventory change, import volumes, and refined product stocks to assess the impact of Sinopec's increased purchases on global balance.
  • Reuters — EXCLUSIVE: Sinopec steps up Russian oil imports — Note the volume figures for Sinopec's Russian oil purchases and the scale of Middle East supply cuts cited by traders and trackers in the report.
  • Reuters — Trump reiterates his positive portrayal of Iran talks — Track the content of Trump's latest statements on Iran talks and any signals regarding the impact of a potential deal on Iranian oil exports.
CRYPTO

Extreme Fear + Negative Coinbase Premium: Do Bitcoin ETF Inflows Reflect Real Demand?

With the Fear & Greed Index at 25 (Extreme Fear), BTC and ETH spot ETFs recorded a combined inflow of $305.2 million on August 5 (IBIT: $196.8M, ETHA: $50.3M). Yet the Coinbase premium has been persistently negative (-0.09) over the past week, and BTC remains in a bearish trend on the 4-hour chart. This contradiction raises a critical question: do ETF inflows reflect genuine retail/institutional buying, or are they driven by arbitrage flows? Key signal to watch: whether the Coinbase premium turns positive and whether daily ETF inflows exceed $400 million.
Primary Sources / Reports:
  • Farside — Bitcoin ETF Flows (All Data) — Review daily inflow/outflow data by fund; identify the concentration of flows in IBIT and FBTC and whether any funds are seeing outflows.
  • Farside — Ethereum ETF Flows — Check ETHA's share of ETH ETF flows and whether the total inflow trend represents a structural break or a one-day spike.
  • Coinglass — Derivatives Data — Monitor BTC open interest (~145M) and funding rates (BTC: 0.0136%); if OI rises alongside ETF inflows, interpret as genuine buying; if OI falls, flag as potential arbitrage pressure.
