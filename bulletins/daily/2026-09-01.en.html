NEUTRAL Macro pressure and selective risk appetite are balancing each other, leaving markets in a neutral regime.

The U.S. 10-year Treasury yield stands at 4.758% after rising 3.8 basis points, while the DXY gained 0.15% to 99.58 and the VIX climbed 6.23% to 15.85. A 0.97% decline in Nasdaq 100 futures signals caution across broad risk assets, although the 0.64% rise in SMH points to relative resilience in semiconductor shares. Brent crude gained 1.90% to $92.21, increasing sensitivity to inflation and interest-rate risks. Total crypto market capitalization fell to $2.63 trillion, down 2.92% over 24 hours; Bitcoin dominance was 59.56%, while Fear & Greed remained in Greed at 69. Bitcoin slipped 0.52% over 24 hours but is still up 23.4% over 30 days; strong ETF inflows and elevated ETH funding are supportive, while a negative Coinbase premium and a slight decline in stablecoin market capitalization argue for caution. Eurozone CPI releases and U.S. ISM, JOLTS, ADP and payroll data are due this week; the next FOMC meeting is scheduled for September 16, with the current 2026 median rate projection at 3.8%.

Is the oil shock becoming a durable inflation and rate shock?

After new Middle East attacks, higher oil prices pushed mortgage rates to their highest level since June 2025, while the U.S. 10-year yield reached 4.758% and MOVE rose to 75.32. The research question is whether the energy shock is merely damaging growth expectations or also narrowing the Fed’s room to ease through inflation expectations; today’s 55.2 ISM manufacturing forecast and the 3.8% daily rise in Treasury yields are the key signals to track together.

Is buying protection genuinely attractive, or an early warning of a volatility premium?

Citadel says the risk-reward of buying equity-market protection is compelling as a historically difficult month begins; VIX rose 6.23% to 15.85 while Nasdaq 100 futures fell 0.97%. The research question is whether the decline reflects broad risk aversion or a narrowing rotation led selectively by Nvidia and CrowdStrike; the divergence between energy’s 2.04% gain and declines in industrials, communication services and real estate is the key cross-check.

CRYPTO

Do ETF inflows and leveraged Ethereum demand tell the same story?

On August 31, Bitcoin ETFs recorded $216.7 million of inflows and Ethereum ETFs $87.6 million, yet total crypto market capitalization fell 2.92% over 24 hours; ETH open interest rose 7.45% and SOL open interest 14.22%. The research question is whether institutional spot demand is producing genuine accumulation or whether rising derivatives leverage is creating a fragile rally; the -0.0259 Coinbase premium and BTC support at 77,447 versus resistance at 79,743 are key confirmation signals.
