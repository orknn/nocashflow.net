RISK-ON Equities, semiconductors, crypto assets and strong ETF inflows show that risk appetite remains firmly alive.

Will Iran tensions create a lasting oil supply premium?

Iran-U.S. tensions, the warning on shutting down Iranian airlines, and the G7 call concerning the Houthis have put geopolitical supply risk back at the center of the oil market; yet energy equities fell while Indian shares benefited from lower oil prices. The key research signal is whether EIA inventory, production, and product-flow data confirm a real supply squeeze and whether oil retains its reaction to further escalation.
