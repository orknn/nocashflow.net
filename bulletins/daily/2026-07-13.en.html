Mon, Jul 13 2026
RISK-OFF Middle East escalation drives oil higher while crypto sentiment remains deep in fear territory.

Markets are trading in a clear risk-off mode today. Iran's escalation of attacks on US bases in Gulf states and the slowdown of Strait of Hormuz traffic to a multi-week low pushed Brent crude up 2.96% to $78.26. The VIX surged 8.3% to 16.28 while the 10-year US Treasury yield holds at 4.57%; the 2Y-10Y spread at +40.9 basis points signals continued curve normalization. On the crypto side, Bitcoin slipped 1.45% to $63,033 over 24 hours, with the Fear & Greed Index at 28 (Fear) confirming weak sentiment and BTC dominance at 56.1% reflecting sustained pressure on altcoins. The macro calendar is critical this week: tomorrow (Tuesday, July 14) at 15:30 brings the US June CPI print, with annual CPI expected to ease from 4.2% to 3.8%. Together with Wednesday's PPI release, this week's inflation data will directly shape expectations for the Fed's July 29 FOMC meeting.
