Global Macro & Crypto Market Intelligence
Tue, Aug 11 2026
RISK-OFF The risk-off regime is reinforced by crypto outflows, rising volatility and geopolitical energy risks.

Market conditions remain defensive: the VIX rose 3.76% to 15.46, while the US 10-year Treasury yield increased 0.84% to 4.70%. Although the DXY was broadly steady at 99.84, a 7.40% decline in the copper-to-gold ratio and a 2.28% drop in SMH point to pressure on growth-sensitive assets. Total crypto market capitalization fell 1.64% to $2.27 trillion, with Bitcoin down 1.6% at $63,865; the Fear & Greed Index stands at 29, in Fear territory, while Bitcoin dominance is 56.51%. Spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded total outflows of $144.6 million, including $53.6 million from IBIT and $40.3 million from FBTC, signaling weaker institutional demand in the short term. US CPI is due on August 12, with headline and core annual forecasts at 3.4% and 2.5%, respectively; August 13 PPI data will also be critical for risk appetite through its impact on inflation expectations and Treasury yields.

MACRO

Do CPI, Treasury yields and Fed pricing point to the same inflation signal?

The August 12 CPI will test how closely inflation moves toward market expectations of 3.4% headline and 2.5% core year over year, versus previous readings of 3.5% and 2.6%. The key research question is how a core monthly print above or below the 0.2% forecast would affect the Fed path and risk assets while the 10-year yield is at 4.699% and the 2-year yield at 4.19%.
Primary Sources / Reports:
  • BLS — Consumer Price Index (CPI) — Review the August 12 headline and core monthly/yearly readings, including shelter and services components, and measure the surprise versus the 0.2% core monthly forecast.
  • US Treasury — Daily Yield Curve Rates — Track the post-CPI direction of the 2-year and 10-year yields from current levels of 4.19% and 4.699%, along with changes in the 2s10s spread.
  • CME FedWatch Tool — Check how the CPI surprise changes rate-cut or higher-for-longer pricing ahead of the September 16, 2026 FOMC meeting.
CRYPTO

Do ETF outflows and a negative Coinbase premium signal a crypto-liquidity regime shift?

Net outflows of $144.6 million from Bitcoin ETFs and $14.6 million from Ethereum ETFs, a 1.64% daily decline in total crypto market capitalization and a Coinbase premium of -0.0599 point to weaker institutional spot demand. Track whether BTC holds the 63,682.2 support or reclaims the 64,693.53 resistance, and whether open-interest declines of 6.66% in BTC and 1.22% in ETH represent leverage cleanup or the start of another selling wave.
