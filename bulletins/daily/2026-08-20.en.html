RISK-ON Falling VIX and MOVE, a powerful crypto rally, and ETF inflows show that risk appetite is clearly dominant.

Global risk appetite remains strong as the VIX fell 5.997% to 14.89, MOVE declined 4.961% to 71.26, and the US 10-year Treasury yield dropped 5.3 basis points to 4.653%. The DXY edged up 0.047% to 98.876, while Nasdaq 100 futures gained 0.311%. Total crypto market capitalization rose 4.898% over 24 hours to $2.385 trillion; BTC gained 7.9%, ETH surged 17.7%, and BTC dominance remained at 58.243%. A Fear & Greed reading of 62 in the Greed zone, a 0.268% increase in total stablecoin market capitalization, and $189.3 million of spot Bitcoin ETF inflows all support demand for risk assets. However, the sharp altcoin advance and rising futures open interest increase the risk of short-term leverage-driven volatility. Today’s calendar features the Philadelphia Fed manufacturing index and US jobless claims at 15:30; the FOMC minutes released during the week remain important for the rate-path outlook.

Is the crypto rally real spot demand or leverage-driven?
