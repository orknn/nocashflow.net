RISK-ON A softer DXY, a stronger copper-to-gold ratio, gains in semiconductors and Greed-level sentiment are supporting risk appetite.

The global backdrop remains RISK_ON despite geopolitical risks and elevated rate volatility. The DXY slipped to 99.22, while the VIX remained subdued at 15.32; the U.S. 10-year Treasury yield was steady at 4.796%. A 3.48% rise in the copper-to-gold ratio and a 0.96% gain in SMH point to continued demand for growth and technology exposure. BTC traded at $77,598, up 1.29% over 24 hours, while total crypto market capitalization stood at $2.61 trillion; however, total capitalization fell 2.44% over 24 hours and BTC was down 2.60% over seven days, highlighting near-term volatility. The Fear & Greed Index at 65 remains in Greed, although BTC dominance at 59.56% suggests risk-taking is still selective. Today’s focus is on U.S. ISM Services PMI and jobless claims, followed tomorrow by payrolls, hourly earnings and the unemployment rate, all of which could shift rate expectations ahead of the Fed’s September 16 FOMC meeting.

The Fed’s September test: weak payrolls versus elevated Treasury yields

The September 4 employment report could reprice the growth-inflation balance ahead of the September 16 Fed meeting, following a prior -23K payroll change and a 4.1% unemployment rate; market data show the 10-year yield at 4.796% and MOVE at 79.71. The research question is whether a rise in wage growth to the 0.3% forecast would offset weak payrolls in rate-cut pricing; the threshold is a payroll result away from the 55K forecast or unemployment outside 4.1%.
