Fri, Aug 7 2026
NEUTRAL Gold and commodity rally reflect geopolitical tensions while crypto holds cautiously ahead of NFP data.

Markets are navigating a careful balance today ahead of the US Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP) release. VIX eased to 15.15 while the 10Y Treasury yield climbed to 4.67%, suggesting markets are pricing in a stronger-than-expected jobs print. The DXY holds near 99.95, barely changed, while gold surged to $4,352 — on track for its best week since January — and silver jumped 4.1% on the day. On the crypto front, BTC trades sideways around $64,258 with the Fear & Greed Index at 29 ('Fear') and total crypto market cap at approximately $2.28T. BTC Dominance stands at 56.6% as most altcoins posted losses over the past 24 hours. Dwindling vessel traffic through the Strait of Hormuz and ongoing Iran-Oman negotiations are adding uncertainty to energy markets, with Brent crude rising 1.4% to $83.61. Overall, the market direction remains ambiguous ahead of NFP, with geopolitical risk continuing to be the primary driver on the commodities side.

NFP Day: 10-Year Yield at 4.67% — Which Scenario Is the Market Pricing In?

Today's (Aug 7) Non-Farm Payrolls release is expected at 85K after the previous 57K print; Average Hourly Earnings and the Unemployment Rate are also due. The 10-year Treasury yield surging to 4.67% (+1.15% daily) and the 2s10s spread widening to 49 bps signal the market is beginning to re-price stagflation risk. The key threshold to watch: if NFP comes in below 85K, will rate-cut expectations for the September FOMC (40 days away) strengthen, or will the 10-year yield push even higher from its current 4.67% level?

Hormuz Risk and Gold's Best Week Since January: Genuine Safe-Haven Buying or Speculative Positioning?

Vessel traffic through the Strait of Hormuz has dwindled noticeably this week as Iran-Oman talks continue, with oil prices rising on concerns about reopening plans. Simultaneously, gold is on track for its best weekly performance since January 2026. The Copper/Gold ratio falling -2.48% on the week signals deteriorating global growth expectations. The critical question: does gold's rally reflect a genuine geopolitical risk premium, or is it temporary positioning tied to pre-NFP dollar weakness? The EIA weekly petroleum report and the trajectory of Hormuz negotiations must be monitored together.

CRYPTO

ETF Inflows Continue While Coinbase Premium Stays Negative: Where Is the Spot Demand?

As of August 6, Bitcoin ETFs saw $137.6M in inflows and Ethereum ETFs $92.1M, both classified as 'Moderate Inflow.' Yet the Coinbase premium has remained persistently negative over the past week (ranging from -0.07 to -0.14), indicating that institutional ETF buying has not translated into US retail spot demand. Combined with a Fear & Greed index reading of 29 (Fear) and a 'Bearish' trend on BTC's 4-hour chart, the critical question is: are ETF inflows genuine accumulation or mechanical arbitrage-driven flows? A Coinbase premium turning positive and BTC breaking the $64,658 resistance level would be a strong recovery signal.
