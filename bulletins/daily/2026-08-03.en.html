RISK-OFF Geopolitical de-escalation eases oil pressure, but rising yields and ETF outflows keep crypto in risk-off territory.

Iran Deal Hopes Sink Oil: How Long Will the Refinery 'Golden Age' Last?

Trump's decision to cancel strikes on Iran and pursue nuclear negotiations sent oil prices sharply lower, while Reuters analysts warn that the refinery margin expansion seen during the conflict period is unlikely to persist. The rapid unwinding of geopolitical risk premium creates a significant divergence between crude producers and refiners, even as the Energy sector (XLE) gained about 1% today. Key signal to watch: EIA weekly crude inventory data — a larger-than-expected build would add further downward pressure on prices, while refiners maintaining cracking spreads could hold a relative advantage.

MACRO

10-Year Treasury at 4.74% in NFP Week: Can Labor Data Corner the Fed?

The US 10-year Treasury yield has risen to 4.74% (daily change +1.76%) and the 2s10s curve has widened to 51 basis points, while ADP employment data is due Wednesday and Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP) on Friday. The previous NFP came in at a weak 57K; the market consensus is 88K. A second consecutive weak print would intensify pressure for a Fed rate cut at the September meeting (44 days away), yet the elevated long-end yield continues to price in a 'stagflation' scenario. Key threshold: an NFP below 70K could pull bond yields lower; above 100K would reinforce the current high-rate environment.
Primary Sources / Reports:
  • BLS — Employment Situation (NFP) — On Friday August 7, evaluate the headline NFP number alongside average hourly earnings (0.3% forecast) and unemployment rate (4.2% forecast) together.
  • CME FedWatch Tool — Track in real time how the probability of a rate cut at the September 2026 FOMC meeting shifts following the NFP release.
  • US Treasury — Daily Yield Curve Rates — Compare 2-year (4.23%) and 10-year (4.74%) yields to monitor the pace and direction of curve steepening; the 60 basis point threshold is critical.

As of July 31, Bitcoin spot ETFs recorded total outflows of $265.4M (IBIT -$122.7M, FBTC -$54.8M), while the Coinbase premium has remained persistently negative over the past week (current: -0.14). The $62,363 level is a critical support for BTC; a break below opens the door to $62,275. Key signal to watch: Are Ethereum ETF flows staying positive (July 31: +$9M), and is this divergence from BTC sustainable?
