Fri, Sep 4 2026
RISK-ON Rising equities, crypto assets and ETF inflows point to strong risk appetite.

Markets are displaying a broad-based advance consistent with a RISK_ON regime: Nasdaq 100 futures rose 0.40%, SMH gained 0.39% and major technology stocks advanced, while the VIX declined to 14.24. The U.S. 10-year Treasury yield fell 3.4 basis points to 4.762%, while a 0.11% rise in the DXY created only modest pressure on risk assets. Total crypto market capitalization increased 1.20% to $2.72 trillion, with BTC up 4.49%, ETH 5.41% and XRP 6.60%; the Fear & Greed index stood at 74, or Greed, and BTC dominance was 59.76%. Brent crude gained 6.76% on the week to $95.35, showing that US-Iran tensions are increasing inflation and supply risks even as risk assets have so far absorbed the shock. Today’s U.S. nonfarm payrolls, average hourly earnings and unemployment data will be crucial for rate expectations and the durability of the rally ahead of the September 16 FOMC decision.

Can the Hormuz risk turn the oil shock into a lasting supply crisis?

The intensifying US-Iran conflict and South Korea’s review of Hormuz options require investors to determine whether oil’s weekly gain reflects a temporary geopolitical premium or expectations of physical supply disruption. The concrete signal to monitor is whether increased military engagement around Hormuz is accompanied by deterioration in EIA inventory, production, or export data.

Will the jobs report reprice the Fed’s September 16 decision?

Today’s payrolls consensus is 55K, with unemployment expected at 4.1% and average hourly earnings at 0.3% month over month, versus a previous employment change of -23K. The research question is whether weak hiring and wage pressure arrive together, and how that combination changes the 4.762% 10-year yield, the DXY, and pricing for the FOMC in 12 days; the key threshold is the report’s deviation from consensus across payrolls, wages, and unemployment.

CRYPTO

Are ETF inflows driving crypto through spot demand or leverage?

Bitcoin ETFs recorded $730.8 million of inflows and Ethereum ETFs $141.4 million on September 3, while total crypto market capitalization rose 1.20% in 24 hours; however, ETH open interest increased 22.73%, SOL funding reached 0.1331%, and the Coinbase premium fell to -0.1357%. The research question is whether strong ETF flows represent durable spot demand or a rally made fragile by derivatives leverage; Bitcoin’s 81,739.995 resistance and 78,721.995 support should be monitored alongside funding, open interest, and the Coinbase premium.
