RISK-ON Falling volatility, tighter credit risk premia and strong crypto flows show that risk appetite is firmly in control.

Risk appetite strengthened across global markets, with Nasdaq 100 futures up 1.16% and SMH gaining 2.88%; NVIDIA led technology with a 9.31% advance. The VIX fell 5.06% to 14.44 and MOVE declined 3.45% to 69.44, signaling easing volatility pressure. The U.S. 10-year Treasury yield stood at 4.66% after a daily change of -0.40 percentage points, while the DXY slipped 0.07%, supporting risk assets. Total crypto market capitalization reached $2.73 trillion, up 0.58% over 24 hours; BTC rose to $80,521, Bitcoin dominance was 59.20%, and Fear & Greed registered 71, or Greed. Core PCE rose 0.2% month over month on August 26, in line with forecasts, while August 27 unemployment claims came in at 203K versus 208K expected; however, Kansas City Fed's Schmid described inflation as stubborn and sticky, preserving a cautious policy risk.
