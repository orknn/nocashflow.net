RISK-OFF Elevated bond volatility, a firmer dollar and weakening risk indicators confirm that markets remain in a risk-off regime.

Global markets are showing a clear risk-off bias: the VIX rose to 17.43, MOVE reached 83.90, and the DXY edged higher to 99.61. The US 10-year Treasury yield remained elevated at 4.96%, while a 3.39% decline in the copper-gold ratio points to weaker growth expectations and risk appetite. Total crypto market capitalization fell 3.96% over 24 hours to $2.62 trillion, with BTC down 1.26% and ETH down 1.61%. Although the Fear & Greed index remains in Greed at 69, a Coinbase premium of -0.158% and falling open interest suggest softer spot demand and reduced leverage appetite. Tomorrow's Fed rate decision, FOMC statement and economic projections, alongside expectations for a policy rate of 4.00%, remain key volatility catalysts amid high yields and geopolitical risks.
